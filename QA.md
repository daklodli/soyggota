# Verification

Automated regression checks cover valid and invalid trip dates, leap days, group limits, blank names, message length, plain-text message formatting, translation completeness, tour images, relative asset URLs, unique IDs, anchor targets and independence from the previous hosting provider.

Browser verification checks the three languages, each tour category, route dialogs, Escape and close buttons, route selection, invalid and valid enquiries, invalidation of a prepared message after edits, clipboard feedback, language persistence and narrow-screen layout.

The enquiry workflow is a message builder, as explained next to the form. External social services and availability depend on their owners. These checks describe the tested behavior; they do not guarantee that every possible browser, device or future third-party change is error-free.

The form now marks missing required fields inline, including a deliberately selected route. The language and route menus support keyboard arrows, Enter and Escape. Coordinate text was removed. The Instagram shortcut opens a chat link and copies text; it does not claim to pre-fill Instagram’s editor.
